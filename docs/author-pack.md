# The author pack

[The author pack](https://github.com/eitansuez/runlab-author-pack) runs a lab on your own computer. You change the lab and try it again there, as many times as you need. You publish it when it is ready.

The lab machine is a Linux computer that runs inside your computer. Your account has to be allowed to start it. The pack runs on a 64-bit PC with Linux. The installer uses apt, the package installer on Debian and Ubuntu, so the computer should be one of those.

The pack is a download. The next page installs it. The pages after that walk through the examples that come in the download. Then you write a lab of your own.

The download also contains a short page of commands. This manual is the longer course.
